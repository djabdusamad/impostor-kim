# Uzbek word bank

19 selectable categories; 534 entries (some intentionally appear in more than one category). Original editorial selection for the game, not a claim that foods or customs belong exclusively to Uzbekistan. Names are neutral game prompts, not endorsements or popularity rankings. Historical and living public figures are mixed where appropriate.

Food and tradition references:

- https://www.youtube.com/@OsmondagiBolalar
- https://green-eco.uz/index.php/GED/article/download/2350/2452/4708 (published research sample of Uzbek creators; follower counts were not used)

Illustrations: 20 original local SVG drawings for common foods and cultural objects. No celebrity portraits or remote tracking images. Other prompts remain text-only. The current round's illustration is sent only to civilians, then is public in results. Offline word-bank files are necessarily public for offline play; no online player receives the chosen word/image through shared lobby state.


Word-specific clues are original single-word game associations. Cultural reference checks: https://ich.unesco.org/en/state/uzbekistan-UZ and https://www.ich.uz/en/ich-of-uzbekistan/national-list/domain-2/287-askiya . Sport reference checks: https://www.olympic.uz/oz/news/olymp-news-13 . hints-source.txt is the editable source for the generated hints.js bank. The same spelling may have different clues by category, especially Rayhon (singer/herb), Paxtakor (club/occupation), and Yor-yor (song/film). Proper names are game prompts, not endorsements.

Hints are exactly one Uzbek word. Custom hints reject spaces, hyphens, multiple words, and the answer itself. Existing multiword custom hints must be edited before they can be used. Words ending in the Uzbek letter g‘ remain valid (for example, Bug‘).
